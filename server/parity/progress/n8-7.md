  Traffic stats/trends skip points with explicit null/unrepresentable counters.

  explicitly asserts all 5 timestamps and complete raw numeric fields, including

  strings containing serialized JSON. Old goldens are untouched.
- Arithmetic fixture changes are named tables: summary 8 rows; traffic 5 rows;
  trend 2 rows. They cover null/NaN/Infinity repair and unrepresentable integers;
  unrelated full results still compare strictly against original Python goldens.
